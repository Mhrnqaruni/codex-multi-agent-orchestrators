of funding. The owner's stricter restriction is stated in our own named terms,
without modifying or mislabeling a standard license.

previously granted elsewhere. They do not claim ownership of independent
outputs, but operating the tool commercially is outside the permission grant.

jurisdiction. These custom terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Qualified
legal review before public distribution is recommended if enforcement is
material. GitHub may display Other instead of a recognized SPDX license; do
not conceal this with an MIT/Apache or open-source badge.
